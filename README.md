# publishing-house
create a thesis publishing house in middle east

What is this about:
You work so hard, prepare your papers and companies say your paper and credentials are rubbish / paper mill.
How to prove you studied?**PUBLISH YOUR PAPER.**

In collaboration with ZYRAZ Education Group *(EduServe) aka http://www.zyraz.college , we will HOST YOUR PAPER and let Google Scholar and robots index it.
Of course, this is 2026 and who don't use AI, at least Co-pilot etc.
We do not go against the norm, but accept it. So we ask honestly, DID YOU USE AI, such as CoPilot , GPT etc.
Admit that you use to Vibecode , help with research etc.
So, we accept your paper, Strip the watermarks if there is, and put to TXT for searchable.

YOU WILL NEED TO LOGIN TO YOUR SCHOOL ACCT to validate your student status. Plus your local GMAIL.

It will be indexed and pushed to http://papers.zyraz.college and indexed and some of my own sites such as blogger etc.

You can attach your sanitised CERTIFICATE *(degree/masters/PhD) also in your profile and if you are in Tech, your links to Github etc.
If you can do a short video of the thesis, will be great too.

It is open to Diploma/Degree/Masters/PhD project papers and if you reference from them, please CREDIT THEM!

Work is in Progress and funding for the project is via Google Ads/ etc
